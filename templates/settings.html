Rebuild Watchlist by Price

Enter your max share price and hit Rebuild. The app scores all {{ universe_count }} stocks in its built-in universe — fetching any that aren't cached yet — then fills your 100-stock watchlist with the best-scoring stocks at or below that price. If there aren't enough, it tops off with the highest-rated picks overall.

This may take a minute or two the first time (same as Refresh Data). Future rebuilds are fast since prices are already cached.
